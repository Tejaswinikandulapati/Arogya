Symptom guide

A rule-based guide to help identify a suitable specialty—not a diagnosis.

Urgent warning signs

If you have severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, fainting, heavy bleeding, or a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services immediately. In India, 112 is the national emergency number.

Do not use this guide to rule out an emergency or delay care.

Lab report checker

Compare one result against a reference interval you enter from your own lab report.

How to use safely

Use the exact reference range and units printed on your lab report. Ranges vary by laboratory, age, sex and other factors.
A value inside the range does not automatically mean everything is healthy; an out-of-range value does not alone establish a diagnosis.
Discuss results with a qualified clinician, especially if you have symptoms or a markedly abnormal result.

Find hospitals

Open a map search for hospitals or clinics near your chosen area.

This opens a map search. It does not verify real-time availability, quality, or emergency capacity.

Emergency care

For a life-threatening emergency in India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a web search.

Everyday health tips

General wellness information for day-to-day routines.
💧

Hydration

Drink fluids regularly; needs vary with activity, weather and medical conditions.

◷

Sleep

Keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule and reduce late-night screen use when possible.

♧

Movement

Build regular movement into your day at a level suitable for your health and abilities.

♡

Medication safety

Follow your clinician's instructions and ask a pharmacist or clinician before changing medicines.

✚

Preventive care

Ask a healthcare professional which screenings and vaccinations are appropriate for you.

☏

Seek advice early

Get professional advice for persistent, worsening or concerning symptoms.
